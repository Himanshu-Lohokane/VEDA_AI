**DSA Grinders: Making DSA Practice Addictive Through Competition**

Every engineering student knows they should practice DSA.  
But most start with motivation, solve a few questions, and disappear after a few days.

The real problem isn’t capability. It’s accountability.

We built DSA Grinders to fix this.

It’s a competitive DSA platform where students can:  
- Sync progress from LeetCode  
- Compete with friends on live leaderboards  
- Join private groups  
- Get daily offensive reminders on WhatsApp  
- Receive savage explicit roasts when they break their streak  

**Point System**  
Easy → 1 pt | Medium → 3 pts | Hard → 6 pts  

What makes it different is peer pressure and accountability. When your friends can see you slacking on the leaderboard, that shame hits different and actually pushes you to stay consistent.

Built by students who suck at DSA, just like you.

Just seeing yourself at the bottom of the leaderboard thrives change.
